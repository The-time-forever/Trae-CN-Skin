- Light: `#edf1f8` field, `#f9fbfe` glass, periwinkle accent `#4c60c4` with magenta-pink `#c2568f` as the second accent.
- Dark: `#0b101d` field, `#151d2f` glass, icy periwinkle accent `#9db8ff` with `#f0a6d6`.

To activate this material, replace the previous active files in `themes/` with `background.png`, `dark.json` and `light.json`, then run `start.bat`.
